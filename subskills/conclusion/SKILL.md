# Conclusion

Read the [main skill](../../SKILL.md). Use the actual method, results, and contributions; the conclusion is not a new research proposal. Recommended length: 150-200 words; hard maximum: 250 words.

**Do not divide this section into subsections.**

## Paragraph Roles

Use the [template guide](references/template-guide.md) for an adaptable LaTeX outline.

Distill the problem and approach, summarize evidence-supported mechanism value, and state the significance in one or a few compact paragraphs. Synthesize problem definition, methodology, and results and discussions.

### Logical Structure

How the research goal was formalized -> analysis and insights -> core innovations and theoretical mechanisms -> experimental comparisons demonstrating advantages -> limitations and future work.

## Writing Rules

- Emphasize what the completed study established, using affirmative confidence proportionate to the evidence.
- Respond to the introduction's objectives and contributions without copying the list or repeating the full architecture or training procedure.
- Explain validated innovative value.
- Synthesize qualitatively. Do not repeat numerical values from figures or tables or add new results, literature claims, or assertions beyond the evidence.
- Connect each mechanism to the corresponding qualitative experimental finding.
- Use no negative phrasing outside the limitations and future work passage.
- Use connectors that accurately express sentence relationships.

## Acceptance and Delivery

Propose revisions and obtain approval before applying them. Include four parts: the complete original conclusion, the complete revised conclusion, the rationale, and supporting passages from the body. Add translations only when requested.

- Keep the logical structure coherent and aligned with this skill.
- Do not repeat experimental numbers or introduce new evidence or unsupported claims.
- Do not copy the contribution list or repeat body text redundantly.
- Avoid empty generalities.
- Use affirmative phrasing outside limitations and future work.
- Connect sentences and clauses accurately.
- Stay within the hard length limit.
